Super Melee 64 — 0.6.0-beta.1 / native14

Source beta for local builds. Public source and downloads:
https://github.com/memelees/super-melee-64

This beta adds a cancellable/resumable source installer, verified official
patch downloads, native Windows launcher, asset-free CI, packet bounds checks,
connection-bound sender admission, and fixed-content co-op without remote
mod/Lua/file exchange. Matching SuperMelee64-native14-beta1 clients are required.

Apple Silicon/macOS 15+ is the primary tested target. Windows x64 is an
experimental beta. No full 120-star, sixteen-person, Internet/NAT or real
Windows playthrough is claimed. Current game-test scope and pass/fail results
are recorded in verification/beta-acceptance.json.

See release/START HERE.txt for installation, controls, saves, co-op, recording
and limitations. See LICENSE, LICENSING.md and THIRD-PARTY.txt for scope and
upstream notices. Only source kits are distributable; never share generated
apps, game assets, disc images, saves or recordings with a source release.
