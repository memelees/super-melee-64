Super Melee 64

A castle adventure with a handpicked Smash roster.

This is a recommended route, not a completed test run. No playthrough was performed to make this guide. Water traversal, cap-dependent objectives, some carried-object interactions and a full 120-star clear still need validation. Keep those as experiments in a recording; do not promise a proven completion route.

Set up a series

Choose Default → New Playthrough for a genuine zero-star save. Use Continue for later sessions; choosing New again creates another save. Press F6 or controller Back / View during gameplay to reopen the Melee-style fighter picker. Choose a fighter, use V, the controller triggers, or the on-screen arrows to cycle costumes, then confirm with Enter / A. Escape / B cancels. Confirming gives a free respawn in the same level; coins, stars, lives and defeated enemies stay intact. Doors, cannons and cutscenes finish before the menu opens. Costume selection is also available at startup. Record ON arms capture at gameplay; F9 starts/stops an MP4 with game audio. Recordings are local. Choose No Melee enemies in Adventure Mode (click it, or cycle with M / controller X) to explore as your fighter with only the original Mario 64 enemies. This works with all save modes and recommended stage starts; only your fighter’s damage panel is shown. Unlocked is useful for isolated showcase episodes, but is a separate save.

Opponents listed with + fight together; → means separate waves with a pause. A KO removes that fighter for the current visit; re-entering the course resets its encounter. NPCs spawn only on safe terrain. Water-only areas may have no eligible spawn. Costume variants advance across repeated appearances and wrap through that fighter’s available costumes.

The recording arc

  1. First eight stars: play Mario or Link. Start with Bob-omb Battlefield stars 1, 2, 4, 6, then Whomp’s Fortress 1, 2, 3, 4. Film Yoshi outdoors, Peach indoors, then DK/Diddy and Mario/Dr. Mario.
  2. First key: enter the 8-star door and beat the original Bowser. Pick up its red-coin star if convenient. Return for cannon objectives after opening the relevant cannons.
  3. Lower-floor episodes: Luigi in the fortress, Ice Climbers on the snow, Ness against Luigi in the haunted house, Young Link at the bay. Use the slide’s finish star as an early bonus. The bay episode is also a water-integration check.
  4. Basement to 30: Sheik in the maze, Samus on the lava, Ganondorf in the sand. Collect Toad rewards as thresholds become available. Catch MIPS at 15 stars if the grab interaction cooperates.
  5. Second key: at 30 stars, clear Board Bowser’s Sub, then Bowser in the Fire Sea. This is the key water-dependent progression checkpoint to validate before promising a full series.
  6. Upstairs to 50: Dedede in Snowman’s Land, Pikachu in Wet-Dry World, Sonic on Tall, Tall Mountain, Kirby on Tiny-Huge Island. Sonic faces Captain Falcon and Tails on the mountain.
  7. Final climb to 70: Fox for the clock’s Mr. Game & Watch/Falco/Wolf gauntlet; Marth for Rainbow Ride’s Falcon/Roy/Zelda duels. Finish the original final Bowser at 70 stars. Save remaining 100-coin, cap and water stars for cleanup episodes.

A concrete 70-star target is 65 course stars marked below, plus the slide finish, its under-21-second star, and the three Toad stars. Substitute Bowser red-coin stars or MIPS if the slide time is awkward. Those extras are recommendations, not assumed unlocks. The gate thresholds remain the original 8 / 30 / 50 / 70.

All 15 secret stars

The slide has two separate entries; every row represents one star. Total: 105 main-course stars + 15 secret stars = 120. Cap switches unlock abilities and are not stars themselves.

Every recommended player is distinct from every opponent in that stage, including later waves. Choose a stage in the game’s character picker with the stage arrows, [ / ], or controller L / R. This selects the recommended fighter and starts directly in that stage with its own progress save, initially with zero stars. Earned stars are retained when you return to the same stage. After collecting a star in a main course, you return directly to its original SM64 star selector with the next mission selected; there is no trip back through the castle. Continue and New Playthrough use the normal castle start.

Recording notes

Begin each episode by showing your fighter and that stage’s opponents. Leave in the KO effect and announcer, then cut between star objectives. Use coins to heal 10 percentage points per coin value. Avoid claiming the recording mode captures your webcam: it records the game and game audio. Keep original save folders backed up before changing builds.
